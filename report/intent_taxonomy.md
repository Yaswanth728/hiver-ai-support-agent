# AmazonHelp Intent Taxonomy

We selected AmazonHelp as the target brand because it has a large
number of support conversations in the Customer Support on Twitter
dataset.

The extracted AmazonHelp conversation dataset contains 136,221
customer-to-AmazonHelp interactions.

## Intents

### 1. delivery_issue

Customer has a problem with delivery, including delayed delivery,
missing packages, incorrect delivery status, or delivery location.

Example:
"My order is at my local courier and still hasn't been delivered."

### 2. order_issue

Customer has a general problem with an order or pre-order that is
not primarily a return, refund, or delivery-status problem.

Example:
"There is still no shipping information for my pre-order."

### 3. refund_billing

Customer is asking about a refund, unexpected charge, or billing issue.

Example:
"I was just charged today. How do I get a refund?"

### 4. return_issue

Customer has a problem involving returning an item, return pickup,
return label, or return status.

Example:
"UPS was supposed to pick up my return package yesterday."

### 5. account_issue

Customer has an issue accessing, closing, managing, or understanding
their Amazon account.

Example:
"I don't remember the email address I used."

### 6. payment_issue

Customer has a payment, wallet, or Amazon Payments problem.

Example:
"The balance was supposed to be added to my wallet, but it wasn't."

### 7. device_technical

Customer has a technical problem with an Amazon device or related
hardware/software such as Kindle, Fire TV, or Echo.

Example:
"My Kindle is not working properly."

### 8. prime_membership

Customer has a problem with Amazon Prime membership, Prime trials,
Prime benefits, or Prime membership status.

Example:
"I'm being charged for Amazon Prime but it says I'm not a member."
